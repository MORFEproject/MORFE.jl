Scanning torsional micromirror — parametric resonance & pull-in

A 1×1 mm² silicon micromirror driven into 1:2 parametric resonance at 24 kHz. The 4-equation ROM captures both the stable scan window and the pull-in cliff, in 0.41 s of wall time vs. 14 hours of FE time-marching.
