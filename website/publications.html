The DPIM method behind MORFE.jl is the result of a decade of work on nonlinear normal forms, invariant manifolds, and FE-native reduction. The list below covers the theoretical foundations, software releases, and applications across MEMS, civil structures, rotating machinery, and shells.

How to cite

If MORFE shows up in your work, please cite the method papers.

The CMAME 2021 paper is the primary reference for the FE-native DPIM algorithm. For high-order and forced extensions, also cite the Nonlinear Dynamics 2022 paper.
